✦ OUR STORY ✦

Old-school soul.
New-school energy.

Vibe Check Cafe is our love letter to the old Irani cafés where everyday conversations felt important.

We borrow marble tables, bentwood chairs, bun maska, strong chai and vintage signs, then give everything a playful modern twist.

Have your nostalgia with Wi-Fi, your Irani chai with a croffle and your old Bombay mood with a very modern playlist.
